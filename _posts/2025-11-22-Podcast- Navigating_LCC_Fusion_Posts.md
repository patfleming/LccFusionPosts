## What this episode covers

### 🗂️ **Two Types of Content on the Posts Site**

The LCC Fusion Posts site has two primary roles:

- **General project posts** — tips, discoveries, short presentations, and updates  
- **Podcast episodes** — each including the slide deck, full dialog transcript, and supporting links  

Every published podcast episode always includes a companion post on this site, making it easy  
to follow along with the slides, diagrams, and references.

---

### 🧭 **Navigating the Posts Website**

Nelson and Harrison explain how to browse the Posts site effectively:

- The **Home page** lists all posts in chronological order  
- Podcast posts are easy to recognize by their slides, embedded video, and full dialog  
- General posts contain updates, tips, and feature announcements  
- Categories and tags make it easy to filter and find what you need  
- Playlists on the LCC Fusion YouTube channel collect all podcast episodes in order  

---

### 🔗 **How the Posts Site Fits into the Fusion Ecosystem**

This episode also describes how the Posts site works together with:

- **The Documentation Site**  
  — your source for technical details, wiring tables, testing, and assembly guides  

- **GitHub Repositories**  
  — source code, schematics, Gerbers, firmware, and design files  

- **The LCC Fusion YouTube Channel**  
  — home of all videos and podcast episodes  
    https://www.youtube.com/@LccFusionProject  

Together, these three resources form a complete learning path:

> **Watch the video → Read the post → Check the documentation.**

---

### 🚀 **Using the Posts Site Effectively**

The podcast highlights a smooth workflow:

1. **Start with the newest posts** to see what’s happening in the project  
2. **Watch the YouTube video** for the high-level overview  
3. **Read the matching post** for slides, dialog, notes, and supporting references  
4. **Jump to the documentation** for pinouts, wiring tables, and technical detail  

The Posts site serves as a bridge between the videos and the documentation,  
helping you learn the Fusion system at your own pace.
